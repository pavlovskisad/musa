# musa and the gold supplier: deal outline

> **Draft for Pav, 2026-11-26. For us; not to be sent as it stands.** Figures are Claude's estimates from typical rates, not researched market prices. Product numbers come from `docs/TREASURY.md` section 11.

## 1. Structure

- **Two sides:** our company (Pav and partner, one entity) and the supplier.
- **Start as a license.** musa (brand, code, design, story) stays ours. The supplier uses it for their market and carries the licence, the customer money and the metal.
- **Then a joint company.** Once volume reaches a level both sides agree, either side can convert the license into a joint company, with our stake set in advance.
- **No setup fee.** The work is counted as our contribution, below.

## 2. Who brings what

| | Brings |
|---|---|
| Us | the product, the brand, taking it live, running it, growth |
| Supplier | metal below spot, prepaid capital, the licence path in Dubai, jewellery, their token later |

## 3. Our contribution

| | Value |
|---|---|
| The product as it stands (app, onboarding, desktop, wallets, testnet contract, admin) | $60–100k |
| Taking it to production (payments, KYC, contract changes, main network, security) | $40–70k |
| Work specific to this supplier (their supply flow, reporting, named as source) | $5–15k |
| Brand (name, `musa.gold`, identity, design language, story) | $30–60k |
| Research and partner materials (bonus and treasury model, schemes, overview page) | $15–30k |
| **Total brought in** | **~$150–275k** |
| Founders' time, at market value | $12–20k a month, ~$150–240k in the first year |

Their own token, when it is ready: an add-on of about $20–40k.

## 4. Running it

**Run and maintenance team (our side)**

| Role | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Full-stack engineer, main (half to full time) | $4–8k, or market value |
| Senior part-timer: security, tech review, backup on call | $3–5k |
| Buyer support (part-time) | $0.5–1.5k |
| Accountant | $0.3–0.8k |
| Infrastructure | $1–1.5k |
| Tools | $0.3–0.6k |
| **Total** | **~$9–17k** |

**Growth team: we run it, they fund it**

| | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Designer (part-time) | $1.5–3k |
| Marketing and community manager | $2–5k |
| Marketing budget | set by them; for example $5–10k for the first three months, judged by cost per buyer |

We run growth because marketing is where the brand lives and the buyer relationship should stay with musa. They fund it because it wins their buyers. Their team can run local channels, Arabic content and jewellery promotion on our brand guidelines.

**Outside the monthly figures**
- Contract audit: $30–80k each time the contract changes, at cost.
- Lawyer: per matter (the license deal, terms of service).
- Pass-through, growing with users: card and bank fees (shown to the buyer at checkout), KYC per check, wallet costs above the free tier.

## 5. How we are paid

- **Run fee** covering the run and maintenance team.
- **Share of volume** on every order. It replaces the setup fee and the value of the brand, so it should come to at least $150–275k over the first two years to beat simply building it for them.
- **Own share of the add-ons:** jewellery store, token front end, Pilgrimage.
- **Stake** on conversion to a joint company.

## 6. Product terms already set

- Target bonus: 1.5% on 6 months, 4% on 12, 10% on 24 (6 / 8 / 10% a year). Funded by the supplier under prepaid supply, paid in grams of their metal.
- Gold delivered evenly from the first day.
- Early exit: the buyer keeps what was delivered, the unearned bonus is not paid, 15% is withheld from the undelivered gold they paid for.
- PAXG first; their token alongside it when ready, then as the default.
- Contract control shared, two of three signatures between us and the supplier.

## 7. To learn in the first meeting

1. What a gram costs them below spot.
2. What money costs them.
3. Who in their group holds or can get the licence.
4. How serious the token and casino bonus plans are, and on what timeline.

## 8. Not decided

- The volume share and the conversion level.
- Who keeps the 15% withheld on early exit.
- The notice period for early exit under prepaid supply.
- Who pays the contract audit.
